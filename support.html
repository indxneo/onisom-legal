Support

Onisom · Last updated 25 July 2026

Onisom is a quiet app, so most questions are about what it is deliberately not doing. Here are the common ones.

Nobody has been paired with me yet

Onisom looks for someone who walks in roughly your time zone. Until it finds a person, it tells you so plainly, and your days still count. You do not need to keep the app open — it keeps looking in the background.

My steps are not being counted

Onisom needs permission to read your step count from Apple Health. If you declined it, the app will tell you on the main screen and show you where to fix it.

Step permissions are not in iOS Settings — Apple keeps them in the Health app. Open Health, then go to Sharing → Apps & Services → Onisom and turn on Steps (and Walking + Running Distance, if you want the Activity tab to show it). Onisom only ever reads from Health; it never writes to it.

Why can I not type my city?

Onisom asks you to pick your city from a list built into the app rather than type it. That list is what turns a city name into the approximate coordinates the weather lookup needs — which is how Onisom can show weather without ever asking for location permission or using GPS. If your city is not on the list, pick the nearest one that is; everything except the weather works exactly the same either way.

What does my companion see about my weather?

Only the condition — clear, rain, snow, and so on. Never your city, never your coordinates, never a temperature. You see the same about them, and only while they have opened the app recently; otherwise your screen simply shows your own sky.

Why is there no chat?

On purpose. Onisom is about knowing someone is out there, not about talking to them. There are no messages, no profiles, no names, and no way for you and your companion to identify each other.

Why are there no step counts on the journey screen?

On purpose. The journey shows one thing only: the number of days you and your companion both walked. No targets, no streaks, nothing to break.

Your own steps and walking distance live in their own place — the Activity tab — which you open when you want them. There is still no goal, no ring and no streak anywhere in Onisom, and you never see your companion’s numbers, only whether a day was shared.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Settings → Delete everything. This permanently erases your account, your city, your step history and your journey, from your device and from our servers. It cannot be undone. See the privacy policy for exactly what is stored.

Still stuck?

Email alishersv.dev@outlook.com and describe what happened. Onisom stores no personal information, so please include your device model and iOS version — there is nothing on our side to look you up by.
