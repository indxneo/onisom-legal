Onisom · Last updated October 12, 2026

Onisom is a quiet app, so most questions are about what it deliberately doesn’t do. Here are the common ones.

What am I actually making?

A picture. Each day you walk enough uncovers a piece of it. When the last piece appears, the picture is yours to keep in Pictures, along with everything else you’ve made. A new journey then begins with a new picture.

A longer walk uncovers more: a single day can uncover up to three pieces. If you walk with someone, a piece appears on the days you both walk, and how many appear depends on how far you both walked.

How do I walk with someone?

Invite them. Go to Settings › Together › Walk with someone, and Onisom gives you a short code to send however you like: a message, an email, or out loud. They open the same screen, tap Enter their code, and type it in. From then on, you’re making one picture together.

Onisom never matches you with a stranger and has no way to find people for you. If you don’t invite anyone, your journey is your own, and it’s not a lesser version. It’s the same picture, made on your own.

Joining someone sets aside the journey you were on. You keep the picture you made so far in Pictures, and you can pick it up again whenever you like.

Do I have to open the app every day?

Ideally, yes, just for a moment. This is the one thing worth knowing about how Onisom works: it reads your steps from Apple Health when you open it, not while it’s closed. If a day goes by without a look, the next time you open Onisom it goes back and counts that day too, as long as it’s within a few days. A day older than that can’t be counted.

So if you walk every day but only open the app once a week, most of those days won’t count. Opening it for a moment is enough; there’s nothing to start, press, or finish. If a day goes by without a look, Onisom will gently mention it on the Today screen. If you’d like a nudge, turn on Settings › Reminder. It’s sent once a day, only on days that haven’t counted yet.

My steps aren’t being counted

Onisom needs permission to read your steps from Apple Health. If it can’t see any, the Today screen will say so and show you what to check. You can also open Settings › Not seeing your steps? at any time.

To check the permission, open the iPhone Settings app and go to Privacy & Security › Health › Onisom. Turn on Steps, and Walking + Running Distance if you want to see how far you went. Onisom only reads from Health and never adds anything to it.

Why isn’t my city on the list?

Onisom has its own list of cities built into the app. You type to search it, then pick your city. The list turns a city name into the approximate coordinates the weather needs, which is how Onisom can show your weather without ever asking for your location or using GPS. It only includes larger cities, so if yours isn’t there, pick the nearest one that is. Everything except the weather works the same either way.

What does my companion see about my weather?

Only the condition, like clear, rain, or snow. Never your city, never your coordinates, and never a temperature. You see the same about them. When their weather isn’t available, your screen just shows your own sky.

Why is there no chat?

On purpose. Onisom is about knowing someone is out there, not about talking to them. There are no messages, no profiles, and no feed. You invited this person, so you know who they are, and you can give them a name in Settings › Together › Their name. That name stays on your iPhone, and they never see it. You never see their step count either, only whether they walked.

What do the numbers on the Today screen mean?

They’re all about you. Today shows your own steps so far, the number of steps that makes a day count, and how far you’ve walked. Above it, your journey shows how many days have counted so far.

You never see your companion’s numbers, only whether they walked. There are no streaks, rings, badges, or leaderboards, and nothing is ever compared with anyone else. Nothing can be lost: a day you miss is simply a day the picture waits.

Can I change the units, the language, or the appearance?

Yes, all three, in Settings.

Units lets you choose °F or °C for the weather, and miles or kilometers for distance. Each has an Automatic option that follows your iPhone’s region and shows which unit that turns out to be. The two are separate on purpose: plenty of people read temperature in one system and walk in the other.

Language offers English, Español, and Русский, or Match iPhone to follow your device. Onisom is written in those three; if your iPhone uses another language, Onisom’s own words appear in English. Your language choice also changes how numbers and dates are written, but not your units, which follow your iPhone’s region. The daily reminder arrives in the language you chose here.

Appearance offers Light, Dark, or Match iPhone.

What happens if I get a new iPhone?

Onisom has no account, sign-in, or password, so your journeys and pictures belong to the iPhone you use it on. They can’t be moved to a new iPhone: on a new iPhone, Onisom starts again with a new journey. Deleting the app and installing it again on the same iPhone keeps everything. Anything left unused is deleted automatically after 90 days, as the privacy policy describes.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings › Delete everything. This permanently erases your journey, the pictures you’ve kept, your city, and your step history, both on your iPhone and on our servers. It can’t be undone. See the privacy policy for exactly what is stored.

Still stuck?

Email alishersv.dev@outlook.com and describe what happened. Onisom stores no personal information, so there’s nothing on our side to look you up by. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.
