Support

Onisom · Last updated 8 August 2026

Onisom is a quiet app, so most questions are about what it is deliberately not doing. Here are the common ones.

Nobody has been paired with me yet

Onisom looks for someone who walks in roughly your time zone. Until it finds a person, it tells you so plainly, and your days still count. You do not need to keep the app open while it searches — but do open it once a day, for the reason below.

Do I have to open the app every day?

Yes, briefly — and this is the one thing worth knowing about how Onisom works. It reads your step count from Apple Health when you open it, not while it is closed. A day on which you never open Onisom is not counted, and it cannot be counted later.

So if you walk every day but only open the app twice a week, only those two days can fill. Opening it for a moment is enough — there is nothing to start, press or finish. If Onisom notices that a day went by without a look, it will quietly remind you on the journey screen.

My steps are not being counted

Onisom needs permission to read your step count from Apple Health. If you declined it, the app will tell you on the main screen and show you where to fix it.

Step permissions are not in iOS Settings — Apple keeps them in the Health app. Open Health, then go to Sharing → Apps & Services → Onisom and turn on Steps (and Walking + Running Distance, if you want the Activity tab to show it). Onisom only ever reads from Health; it never writes to it.

Why can I not type my city?

Onisom asks you to pick your city from a list built into the app rather than type it. That list is what turns a city name into the approximate coordinates the weather lookup needs — which is how Onisom can show weather without ever asking for location permission or using GPS. If your city is not on the list, pick the nearest one that is; everything except the weather works exactly the same either way.

What does my companion see about my weather?

Only the condition — clear, rain, snow, and so on. Never your city, never your coordinates, never a temperature. You see the same about them, and only while they have opened the app recently; otherwise your screen simply shows your own sky.

Why is there no chat?

On purpose. Onisom is about knowing someone is out there, not about talking to them. There are no messages, no profiles, no names, and no way for you and your companion to identify each other.

What do the numbers on the journey screen mean?

There are only two, and both are about you. The first is the count of days you and your companion both walked — that is the journey, and it is the only thing that adds up over time. The second appears on the day’s card until you have walked far enough: your own step count so far, and the number of steps that fills a day.

Once the day is counted, the figure goes away and the card simply says you have walked. There are no streaks, no rings, no badges and no leaderboards, and nothing is ever compared with anyone else.

Your own steps and walking distance also live in their own place — the Activity tab — which you open when you want them. You never see your companion’s numbers at all, only whether a day was shared.

Can I change the units, the language or the appearance?

Yes, all three, in Settings.

Units lets you pick °C or °F for the weather, and kilometres or miles for distance. Each has an Automatic option, which follows the region your iPhone is set to and tells you which unit that works out as. The two are separate on purpose: plenty of places read temperature in Celsius and walk in miles.

Language offers English, Español and Русский, or Match iPhone to follow your device. Onisom is written in those three; on a device set to any other language you will see that language’s system wording and English for Onisom’s own words. Choosing a language here also changes how numbers and dates are written, but it does not change your units — those follow your iPhone’s region, because the language you read is not necessarily the region you live in.

Appearance offers Light, Dark, or Match iPhone.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Settings → Delete everything. This permanently erases your account, your city, your step history and your journey, from your device and from our servers. It cannot be undone. See the privacy policy for exactly what is stored.

Still stuck?

Email alishersv.dev@outlook.com and describe what happened. Onisom stores no personal information, so please include your device model and iOS version — there is nothing on our side to look you up by.
