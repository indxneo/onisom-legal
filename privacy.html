Privacy Policy

Onisom · Last updated 25 July 2026

Onisom pairs you anonymously with one other walker and shows you a shared tally of the days you both walked. It is built so that we know as little about you as possible. There is no account to create, no profile, and no way for you and your companion to identify each other.

What Onisom stores

  • An anonymous identifier. A random ID is created for you on first launch. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number, or Apple ID, and you never enter any of those.
  • Your city. Chosen during setup from a list built into the app. Used to show the weather where you are walking, and to send that weather — the condition only, such as “rain” — to your companion. Only the city name is stored. Onisom never asks for location permission, never uses GPS, and never knows where you actually are.
  • Your time zone. Read from your device, used to pair you with someone who walks at roughly the same hours as you.
  • A daily step count. One number per day, read from Apple Health. Onisom reads this and never writes to Health. It does not read routes, workouts, heart rate, or any other health data.
  • A notification token. Only if you allow notifications. Used solely to tell you that your companion walked.
  • Your shared journey. Which anonymous companion you are paired with and how many days you have both walked.

What Onisom does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or photograph.
  • It does not collect your precise location or any GPS data.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking software.
  • It does not sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.
  • It does not let you and your companion message each other or see each other's identity, city, or step counts. You each see the other's weather — one of a few conditions like clear, rain, or snow — and nothing that says where that weather is.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase hosts the database that stores the data listed above.
  • Apple Push Notification service delivers notifications, if you enabled them.
  • Open-Meteo provides weather. It receives only the approximate coordinates of the city you chose, taken from the list built into the app — the same coordinates for everyone who picks that city. It never receives your identifier, your device, or anything else about you.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything at any time from inside the app: Settings → Delete everything. This permanently removes your identifier, your city, your step history, your notification token, and your journey, from your device and from our servers. It cannot be undone.

If you stop using Onisom, your account and all of the data above are deleted automatically after 90 days of inactivity.

Children

Onisom is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
